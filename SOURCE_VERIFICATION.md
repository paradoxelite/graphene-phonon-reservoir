# Source verification

DOI and title metadata below were checked against the Crossref Works API on 2026-08-14. These sources motivate model families; none validates the parameters or results of this repository.

| DOI | Registered title | Role here | Does **not** establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| `10.1016/j.neunet.2019.03.005` | *Recent advances in physical reservoir computing: A review* | Context for physical reservoir computing | Advantage of this implementation |
| `10.1063/1.5038038` | *Reservoir computing with a single delay-coupled non-linear mechanical oscillator* | Prior mechanical reservoir example | Equivalence to coupled graphene modes |
| `10.1126/science.1136836` | *Electromechanical Resonators from Graphene Sheets* | Existence of graphene resonators | This geometry, mass, Q or voltage |
| `10.1038/nnano.2011.71` | *Nonlinear damping in mechanical resonators made from carbon nanotubes and graphene* | Motivation for nonlinear damping | The coefficient used here |
| `10.1039/D1NA00794G` | *Fabry–Perot interferometric calibration of van der Waals material-based nanomechanical resonators* | Context for optical interferometry | A calibrated readout architecture here |
| `10.1038/srep00514` | *Information Processing Capacity of Dynamical Systems* | Context for capacity concepts | A universal capacity budget for this model |

An initially guessed DOI for the Dion article (`10.1063/1.5042342`) was rejected because Crossref resolves it to *Tutorial: Photonic neural networks in delay systems*. The report uses the corrected DOI `10.1063/1.5038038`.
